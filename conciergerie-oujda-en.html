Frequently asked questions from owners in Oujda

Who collects the booking payments?

The owner, always. Payments arrive in their account. Azur Stay never touches the funds; a detailed statement is provided regularly and the commission is settled after payment is received.

What is the commission based on?

On the net income after platform fees, never on the gross amount.

Who pays for cleaning and linen?

Charged back to the guest: a neutral cost for the owner.

Who pays for repairs and maintenance work?

Costs remain the owner's responsibility. A quote is drawn up and sent, the owner approves before anything is committed: Azur Stay never advances the funds.

Am I tied into a fixed term?

We start with a trial period. The owner remains free.

I live abroad, how does it work?

This is our core expertise with Moroccans abroad: our local team handles everything on site, and you track your income remotely. Everything is arranged via WhatsApp, in French or Arabic.

What types of properties do you manage in Oujda?

Houses, villas or apartments, across every segment: long-term stays, professionals on assignment, families or short stays. Oujda has demand year-round, unlike a seasonal destination.

Why Azur Stay rather than a local agency?

We are not improvising: we have been operating and managing apartments in Saïdia for 10 years, with a professional setup (channel manager, secure payments, transparent reporting). The same method is now deployed in Oujda, with a team already in place.

Are you really based in Oujda, or do you manage remotely from Saïdia?

We have a local team based in Oujda year-round for cleaning, maintenance and technical work, with regular on-site supervision. This is not remote, improvised management.
